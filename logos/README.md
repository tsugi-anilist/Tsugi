# Other sites' icons (0.12.4)

Each site's own icon, unchanged (not redrawn, recolored or cropped), shown only beside that site's score on
a title page, to say where the number comes from, with a link to the title there, and on its toggle in Settings.
Since 0.12.7 also on links to those sites: MyAnimeList's and Reddit's in AniList's "External & Streaming links",
and AniList's own on MyAnimeList's title pages (`myanimelist.svg`, `reddit.png` and `anilist.png` are web-accessible
there, manifest `web_accessible_resources`).
Tsugi never uses them as its own mark or in its name. If a site asks for its icon or its data to be taken out, it goes. The last four sites' scores aren't asked from them: they come
in MangaBaka's answer (its `source` field, manga only), and are off until switched on. Downloaded on 2026-11-13:
